# ADR-0005: Mobile-first Responsive Strategy

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-07

## Context

Wayly is designed for users who are on the move and need a clear, decision-oriented experience while traveling or in a city context. Mobile is the primary context, not a reduced version of desktop.

## Decision

Design the experience as mobile-first and progressively enhance it for larger screens.

## Consequences

- faster iteration on the primary use case;
- better UX for real-world mobility scenarios;
- improved accessibility and clarity on small screens;
- more explicit responsive design decisions.

## Notes

The UX is tailored to on-the-go usage and should remain usable on tablets and desktop as a later enhancement.
